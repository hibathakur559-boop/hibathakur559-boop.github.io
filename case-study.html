Case Study

Ranking What to Fix First

The problem

FlyRank's content teams have far more pages than time to review. With limited weekly capacity, reviewers needed a way to know which pages to look at first — not a dashboard of every metric, and not a black-box score they couldn't question.

What I did (and what I decided)

I built a ranked scoring system on FlyRank's anonymized search performance data. I started with a transparent baseline rule before touching a model, then trained a Random Forest classifier and deliberately validated it under a client-grouped split, so no client's pages could appear in both training and testing.

That choice mattered: a naive random split reported a misleadingly high 94% precision, while the honest split brought it down to a real 74% — still a genuine improvement over the 66% baseline, just not an inflated one. I also ran a full leakage audit and rewrote my own boldest claims into safer, evidence-backed language before calling the work done.
